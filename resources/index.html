03 · Output folders

Keep sources, transforms, masks, outputs, and QA separate

The structure below mirrors the worked example while making it clear which products live in MNI space and which are participant-specific.

project/ └── derivatives/ └── mesoconnect/ └── sub-1006/ ├── xfm/ │ └── standard2acpc_dc.nii.gz ├── atlas_mni/ │ ├── left_inferior_vta_nac_mni152_1mm_thr50.nii.gz │ ├── right_inferior_vta_nac_mni152_1mm_thr50.nii.gz │ ├── left_inferior_vta_nac_mni152_1mm_thr50_dil2mm.nii.gz │ ├── right_inferior_vta_nac_mni152_1mm_thr50_dil2mm.nii.gz │ └── inferior_vta_accumbens_inclusion_corridor.nii.gz ├── roi_native/ │ ├── left_vta.nii.gz │ ├── right_vta.nii.gz │ ├── left_accumbens.nii.gz │ ├── right_accumbens.nii.gz │ ├── inferior_vta_accumbens_inclusion_corridor.nii.gz │ ├── inferior_vta_accumbens_exclusion_corridor.nii.gz │ ├── T1_MNI_Lhemi_bin.nii.gz │ └── T1_MNI_Rhemi_bin.nii.gz ├── tractography/ │ ├── left_inferior_vta_nac.tck │ └── right_inferior_vta_nac.tck ├── qa/ │ ├── sub-1006_corridor_overlay.png │ └── sub-1006_inferior_vta_nac_streamline_qc.png ├── commands/ │ └── sub-1006_tractography_command.txt └── manifest.tsv

The manifest should include atlas version, threshold, dilation kernel and radius, transform and interpolation, every seed, target, waypoint, and exclusion mask, FOD and gradient inputs, streamline and attempt targets, cutoff, stop rule, software versions, and deviations from the reference workflow.

04 · Troubleshooting

Common failures and first checks

ProblemLikely causeFirst checks
Warped atlas is shiftedPoor registration, coverage mismatch, or wrong transform direction.Overlay T1 and MNI images; verify skull stripping, brain coverage, warp direction, and reference grid.
Left/right appears flippedHeader orientation issue or mislabeled hemisphere.Inspect fslhd/fslorient output and overlay known asymmetric anatomy.
Mask vanishes after warpingWrong transform, output grid, or interpolation.Confirm MNI-to-native direction, nonempty input, native reference, and --interp=nn.
Mask enters ventricles or gray matterRegistration error or excessive dilation.Inspect the undilated and dilated atlas; correct alignment before reducing anatomical constraints.
Corridor tracking failsCorridor does not connect seed and target or exclusion is too restrictive.Overlay every mask together; inspect the inverse and hemisphere-mask orientation.
Few or zero streamlinesIncompatible masks, geometry, cutoff, or tracking parameters.Confirm reachability within the corridor and compare the full command with pathway-specific settings.

05 · QA checklist

Minimum evidence to retain

  • Atlas release version, pathway, hemisphere, and participant-overlap threshold.
  • Undilated and dilated MNI-space atlas overlays.
  • Native-space corridor and endpoint-region overlays.
  • Evidence that the transformed corridor remains connected to seed and target.
  • At least one corridor overlay and one streamline QC image per participant.
  • Accepted streamline count and whether the maximum seeding attempts were reached.
  • Exact command, software versions, and all deviations from the reference workflow.
  • Reconstruction success and missingness by pathway, hemisphere, and participant for group analyses.

06 · Citation

Cite the manuscript and the atlas release

Cite the final MesoConnect manuscript and the exact versioned atlas release used in your analysis. The manuscript title in the supplied draft is:

Mapping Mesolimbic Dopamine Projections to Subcortical Circuits Underlying Adaptive Behavior: A Human 7T Diffusion Tractography Atlas

Blake L. Elliott, Ranesh Mopuru, Linda J. Hoffman, Josiah K. Leong, Nora D. Volkow, Ingrid R. Olson, and Vishnu P. Murty.

Add the final journal citation, DOI, repository URL, archived release identifier, and atlas version after they are confirmed. The repository uses scoped terms: HCP data-use terms for derived atlas data, CC BY 4.0 for original documentation and figures, and MIT for original code. See the license notice.
