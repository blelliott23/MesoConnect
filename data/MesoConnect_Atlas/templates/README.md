# FSL MNI152 1 mm brain template

`MNI152_T1_1mm_brain.nii.gz` is the skull-stripped 1 mm MNI152 T1 template copied from:

```text
$FSLDIR/data/standard/MNI152_T1_1mm_brain.nii.gz
```

File verification:

- Matrix: 182 × 218 × 182 voxels
- Resolution: 1 × 1 × 1 mm
