Atlas at a glance

Seven pathways, three functional groupings

The groupings organize the atlas for navigation. They do not imply a fixed one-pathway, one-function mapping; the pathways are overlapping components of a broader circuit.

Downward arc

Regulation of motivated behavior

Two separately reconstructed components of the hippocampus–VTA regulatory circuit.

  • HPC–NAc–VP
  • VP–VTA
Upward arc

Motivated memory

VTA-centered trajectories approaching distinct anterior and posterior hippocampal territories.

  • VTA–Anterior HPC
  • VTA–Posterior HPC
Direct pathways

Motivated salience and learning

Target-defined VTA trajectories to accumbal and amygdala territories.

  • Inferior VTA–NAc
  • Superior VTA–NAc
  • VTA–Amygdala

From participants to atlas

How MesoConnect was built

Native-space tractograms were converted to voxel maps, transformed to MNI space, aggregated across participants, and thresholded at multiple participant-overlap levels.

  1. Streamlines in native space
  2. Voxel-wise tract maps
  3. Binarized maps in MNI space
  4. Group overlap probability
  5. Thresholded atlas maps
